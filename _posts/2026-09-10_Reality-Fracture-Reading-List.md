There's been a lot of buzz about the Reality Fracture story, and let me tell you—it's fantastic. Haven't been reading magic story and want to know how to catch up? I'm here to help!

Reality Fracture will cap off a 3-year (12 set) story which is split into three sub-arcs.

## Reality Fracture Arcs

The Omenpath Arc

- Wilds of Eldraine
- Lost Caverns of Ixalan
- Murders at Karlov Manor
- Outlaws of Thunder Junction

The Dragonstorm Arc

- Bloomburrow
- Duskmourn: House of Horror
- Aetherdrift
- Tarkir: Dragonstorm

The Echoverse Arc

- Edge of Eternities
- Lorwyn Eclipsed
- Secrets of Strixhaven
- Reality Fracture

## What to read?

If you want to fully catch up, read the main stories for all 12 of the sets. All of them can be found in [the story archive on the mothership](<https://magic.wizards.com/en/story#archive>)—it's sorted by year, Wilds of Eldraine starts in 2023. The earlier sets in each arc (e.g Wilds of Eldraine, Bloomburrow, Edge of Eternities) tend to be more disconnected from the larger arc, whereas the final sets in each arc (Outlaws of Thunder Junction and Tarkir: Dragonstorm) tend to focus more on progressing the overarching story. You can read the side stories also, but they tend to not have overarching story relevance. They're often fantastic bits of worldbuilding though!

### The Essentials

If you want to minimize the amount you read, I believe the following are mandatory for understanding what's going on:

- Outlaws of Thunder Junction Epilogue: [Part 1](<https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/epilogue-1-the-invasion-tree>) and [Part 2](<https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/epilogue-2-bring-the-end-part-2>)
- Aetherdrift: [Episode 6](<https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/aetherdrift-episode-6-the-bloodless-revolution>)
- Tarkir Dragonstorm: [Episode 6](<https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/tarkir-dragonstorm-episode-6-how-wretched-love>)
- Secrets of Strixhaven: [Episode 6](<https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/secrets-of-strixhaven-episode-6-two-three-five-seven>)
- Secrets of Strixhaven Side Story: [The Math of More](<https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/secrets-of-strixhaven-the-math-of-more>)

### Chef's Special

Naturally, I'd recommend reading the stories for OTJ, DFT, TDM, and SOS in full, but the above linked episodes are the crucial ones. Side stories aren't usually connected to the overarching plotline, but The Math of More sets up a lot of concepts and themes that Reality Fracture Episode 1 seems to building on. Edge of Eternities is very disconnected and for now it is skippable, but it may provide important context for Reality Fracture, we don't know yet.

### Background Reading

For important Jace background, you should ideally also read [Absent Minds](<https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/feature/jaces-origin-absent-minds-2015-06-24>) from Origins and the Ixalan arc starting with [Jace, Alone](<https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/jace-alone-2017-09-06>). I would also recommend the MOM side story [Ravnica: One and the Same](<https://magic.wizards.com/en/news/magic-story/one-and-the-same>) for Vraska/Jace context, and as a bit of a prologue to OTJ epilogue.

### The SparkNotes

Alternatively, you could also listen to [The Vorthos Cast's Jummary](<https://soundcloud.com/user-345643028/388-the-jummary-jace-summary>), which is basically a crash course. If you go this route, I do still recommend reading OTJ Epilogue in its entirety though.
