We've speculated about the Foundway Associates from pretty early on, and when the Planeswalker's Guide was published without mention, it was pretty reliably going to be puzzle related. Not that we guessed that it would be a crossword though

A pretty straightforward puzzle again. The blurb hints you pretty hard at the Foundway Associates, and you'll notice there are [12 of them](https://scryfall.com/search?q=set%3Amkm+ft%3Afoundway) and 12 rows/columns.
